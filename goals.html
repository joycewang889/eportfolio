Career Goals

Career goals

I want to work in hardware. I already have hands-on physical design experience running RTL-to-GDS flows in Cadence Innovus, and I want to build on that with a hardware internship in Summer 2027.

What I'm looking for

I'm open to any hardware role. The areas I'm applying to:

  • ASIC design and verification. RTL, UVM testbenches, assertions and coverage, like my calculator ASIC and RISC-V CPU.
  • Physical design. Floorplanning, place and route, timing closure and DRC, which is what I do at Silicon Jackets.
  • Hardware test and board-level design. Schematics, PCB layout, bring-up and lab debug, like the EMG flex board and my Wheatstone bridge build.

I like seeing a design go from an idea to something physical that works, and figuring out why it doesn't work yet. An internship would show me how a real team does that on real products.
