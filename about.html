About

About me

I was born in China and moved to the U.S. as a kid. I grew up in Irvine, California, went to Northwood High School, and now I'm studying Electrical Engineering at Georgia Tech. I speak English and Mandarin.

How I got into EE

In high school I was technical lead for my team in NASA Student Launch, where we flew a high-power rocket to about a mile. I also did a research project on a car suspension that turns bouncing into electricity. Both projects ended up being a lot of electronics: sensors, control, and making sure the data was right.

At Georgia Tech I found chip design through Silicon Jackets. I like writing RTL, seeing it turn into a real layout, and fixing timing and DRC problems until it comes back clean. My threads are Signal & Information Processing and Circuit Technology.

At Georgia Tech

Silicon Jackets

Physical design engineer on GT's chip design team. I run RTL-to-GDS flows in Cadence Innovus: floorplanning, clock tree synthesis, routing, timing closure and DRC fixes.

GAMMA lab

Simulink models of analog and mixed-signal circuits, and a MATLAB optimizer that sizes OTA amplifiers.

VIP

Mechatronics & Motivation. I co-designed and hand-assembled a wearable EMG board on a flexible PCB.

RHA

Meetings Coordinator for the Residence Hall Association. I ran weekly meetings, spent RHA funds on things residents voted for (volleyball nets, lamps), and helped run events like a Thanksgiving dinner and a Super Bowl watch party.

Tutoring

Registered peer tutor at GT for math, physics and CS.

Teaching

I've tutored for more than five years. I also run my own bilingual tutoring business for families around Gwinnett County, mostly SAT prep, school math, reading and ESL. I make each student's worksheets and practice sets from scratch based on what they need to work on.

In high school I taught weekly Scratch coding classes with canCode to about 30 kids in underserved communities.

Outside engineering

Golf and tennis

I've played golf for about 10 years. I played varsity all through high school and played international junior tours, including runner-up on the Southern California PGA Players' Tour in 2022. I also played varsity tennis.

Guzheng 古筝

The guzheng is a 21-string Chinese zither. You tune it by sliding a bridge under each string and pluck with picks taped to your fingers. I've played since I was little, earned the Central Conservatory of Music Level 8 certificate (out of 9), and performed with the California Youth Chinese Symphony.
